# Weekly Topics — Week 22, May 2026

## Paperclip — Short Description

Paperclip is an open-source platform for managing and orchestrating teams of AI agents as if they were employees in a company. Instead of running isolated agents, it provides organizational structures such as roles, reporting hierarchies, goals, budgets, governance, and task coordination, allowing users to operate AI-powered teams from a single dashboard. It is self-hosted, model-agnostic, and works with existing agent frameworks like Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenClaw, and custom agents.

## Key Angles to Cover

- AI Agents as a Company
  Core concept: "If an agent is an employee, Paperclip is the company."
  Organizes agents into departments, teams, and reporting structures.
  Moves from prompt-based interaction to management-based interaction.
- Multi-Agent Orchestration
  Coordinates multiple specialized agents toward a shared objective.
  Handles delegation, collaboration, and task routing.
  Supports cross-functional workflows (engineering, marketing, research, operations).
- Goal-Driven Execution
  Work is aligned to company missions and project goals.
  Tasks roll up to higher-level business outcomes.
  Focuses on business objectives rather than individual prompts or tickets.
- Governance & Human Oversight
  Human acts as the board of directors.
  Approval workflows for hiring agents, strategy changes, and budget increases.
  Full control over agent autonomy.
- Budget & Cost Management
  Agent-level budgets and spending controls.
  Automatic pauses when limits are reached.
  Visibility into AI operating costs.
- Model & Framework Agnostic
  Works with different AI models and runtimes.
  No vendor lock-in.
  Can integrate existing agent ecosystems instead of replacing them.
- Operational Transparency
  Audit trails, task history, decision logs, and traceability.
  Visibility into what agents are doing and why.
  Designed for accountability and debugging.
- Open Source & Self-Hosted
  MIT-licensed and self-hosted.
  Organizations retain control over data, infrastructure, and model choices.
  Suitable for enterprises concerned about compliance and ownership.
- "Zero-Human Company" Vision
  Ambitious vision of businesses run largely by AI agent teams.
  Positions itself as infrastructure for autonomous organizations.
  Useful discussion point for future-of-work and agentic AI conversations.
- Enterprise Use Cases
  Content operations
  Software development teams
  Research and competitive intelligence
  Customer support workflows
  Marketing automation
  Internal operations and process automation

## One-Line Positioning

Paperclip is an operating system for AI organizations, providing the structure, governance, and coordination needed to turn individual AI agents into productive teams.
